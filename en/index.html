Featured · live demo

CycleGen — driving cycles from a map

Draw a route on a map and CycleGen turns it into a realistic speed profile (gradient, bends, traffic lights, traffic), then the wheel forces for the vehicle you pick. It is how I took vehicle modelling into an actual web product.
